About
=====

Climate Intelligence is the blog of **J. Xavier Prochaska**, a professor at the
University of California, Santa Cruz. He is its founder and, for now, its sole
author.

The blog is about the science of the climate, biodiversity, global population,
the unhoused, and artificial intelligence. Its premise is that two exponentials
dominate our future, the climate crisis and AI, and that most of us are poorly
equipped to reason about either. The posts try to do that reasoning carefully,
from primary sources: the IPCC's sixth assessment cycle, Tom Murphy's *Energy
and Human Ambitions on a Finite Planet*, the work of James Hansen, the
Stockholm Resilience Centre's planetary-boundaries framework, and the peer
reviewed literature behind each figure.

Artificial intelligence is both a subject here and a collaborator. Each post is
written by the author, with figures built together with Claude (Anthropic),
and every exchange with the AI is logged and its energy cost published. See
:doc:`How it's made <how-its-made>` and :doc:`Our footprint <footprint>`.

The six :doc:`guiding principles <principles>` set the editorial bar.
